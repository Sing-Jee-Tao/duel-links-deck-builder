Dark Magician Control

TIER 2 · KOG VIABLE
READ TIME 4 MIN

Game plan

You are not racing to a combo. The deck wins by keeping the board flat and the opponent's hand empty until turn four, then closing with a single 2500 body that the opponent has already spent their answers on.

Three monster zones means one resolved backrow answer usually decides the turn. Hold Enemy Controller for the summon that actually threatens lethal rather than the first one you see, and remember there is no Main Phase 2 to bail you out of a bad attack.

Opening priorities

01 Set Wall of Disruption on any hand with two or more monsters. It answers the swarm turn that this deck otherwise loses to outright.
02 Keep Sphere Kuriboh in hand through turn two. It is your only Limited 1 slot, and discarding it for a marginal tribute wastes the whole allowance.

Key interactions

Book of Moon flips your own Dark Magician face-down to dodge targeted removal, then re-summons it as a fresh normal summon next turn.
Cosmic Cyclone banishes rather than destroys, which beats graveyard triggers but costs 1000 of your 4000 life points. Do not fire it on turn one against a burn deck.

Common matchups

Blue-Eyes Chaos MAX 42% Their 4000 ATK ends you in one swing. Wall of Disruption is the only reliable out; hold it.
Six Samurai 58% They flood three zones early, which is exactly what Wall of Disruption taxes. Let them commit first.

Ruling · Book of Moon

Flipping a monster face-down resets its effects and clears any equip or continuous targeting it. The monster loses its summon status, so a face-down Dark Magician can be normal summoned again on a later turn.
